Moonlight Sonata × Fantaisie-Impromptu: The Midnight Hour (Piano Fusion)

Two minutes to midnight, and the old clock tower has started to run away. Beethoven's Moonlight finale, Chopin's Fantaisie-Impromptu and Rachmaninoff's C-sharp minor Prelude, joined in one key. About three and a half minutes.

Watch on YouTube

About this piece

A fusion of public-domain works: Beethoven, Moonlight Sonata, 3rd movement; Chopin, Fantaisie-Impromptu; Rachmaninoff, Prelude in C-sharp minor, Op. 3 No. 2. The passages were chosen and joined by software, arranged and played on a sampled piano, with a person directing the process and listening. The story and the thumbnail artwork were made with AI.
