About this piece

A new arrangement that combines music from three public-domain works: Satie's Gymnopédie No. 1, the Prelude in C major from Bach's Well-Tempered Clavier, and Debussy's Clair de Lune. It was arranged and played by software on a sampled piano, with a person directing the process and listening to every version.
