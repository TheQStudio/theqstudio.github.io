Falling for You (A Love Story in Piano)

Wonder, the chase, rejection and a happy ending. About five minutes.

Watch on YouTube

About this piece

A new arrangement that tells a love story in six scenes, combining music from four public-domain composers. It was arranged and played by software on a sampled piano, with a person directing the process and listening to every version.

Chapters

0:00 First sight (Debussy, Clair de Lune)
0:53 Infatuation (Schumann, Träumerei)
1:28 The chase (Mozart, Rondo alla Turca)
2:09 Rejection (Beethoven, Pathétique, Grave)
2:39 Persistence (Beethoven, Pathétique, Rondo)
3:40 She says yes (Beethoven, Pathétique, Adagio)
4:26 Together (Debussy, Clair de Lune)

Music from

  • Claude Debussy: Clair de Lune
  • Robert Schumann: Träumerei (Kinderszenen)
  • Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart: Rondo alla Turca (Sonata K. 331)
  • Ludwig van Beethoven: Piano Sonata No. 8 "Pathétique"
