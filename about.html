About

TheQStudio makes quiet piano videos for listening, studying and learning to read music. Each one shows a keyboard with falling notes and the sheet music above it, so you can see where the music is. The C keys are labelled, so you can find your place on a real piano.

Made with software

The music is arranged and played by software, on a sampled piano, not recorded by a pianist. The scores come from public-domain composers such as Bach, Beethoven, Chopin, Debussy and Satie. Software chooses how to combine and shape them, and a person directs it and listens to every version, changing what does not sound right.

Because the notes are from the original composers, they are free for anyone to use. The arrangement, performance settings and video are made fresh for each piece.
